Was ist BeatIQ?

Höre kurze Musikclips und rate Titel, Artist und Erscheinungsjahr. Jedes Quiz besteht aus 5 Runden mit kürzer werdenden Clips (30 → 5 Sekunden). Je schneller du rätst, desto mehr Punkte bekommst du. Sammle XP, steige Level auf und schalte Achievements frei.

Features

🎵

3 Kategorien

Pop & Rock, Film & TV und Klassik — jeweils mit wählbaren Jahrzehnten oder Epochen.

Die Clips werden kürzer (30 → 22 → 15 → 10 → 5 Sek.). Runde 5 ist der Expert-Modus mit doppelten Punkten. Läuft die Zeit ab, ist die Runde verloren.

🔥

What is BeatIQ?

Listen to short music clips and guess the title, artist, and year. Each quiz has 5 rounds with progressively shorter clips (30 → 5 seconds). The faster you answer, the more points you earn. Collect XP, level up, and unlock achievements.

Features

🎵

3 Categories

Pop & Rock, Film & TV, and Classical — each with selectable decades or epochs.

⏱

5 Rounds, Rising Difficulty

Clips get shorter each round (30 → 22 → 15 → 10 → 5 sec). Round 5 is Expert mode with double points. If the timer runs out, the round is lost.

⚡️

Speed Bonus

Up to 50 extra points for fast answers — but only when all three fields are correct.

🔥

Streak Multiplier

3 correct answers in a row multiplies your points by 1.5×. Expert mode applies 2×.

🏆

Achievements

Unlockable achievements for special accomplishments. Can you get them all?

📈

XP & Levels

Your progress is saved. Level up and become a music expert.

🔒 No Tracking. No Ads.

BeatIQ collects no personal data. No account required. Your progress stays on your device.
